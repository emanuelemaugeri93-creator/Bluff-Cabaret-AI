Story Behind the Cards Booklet Forge

Load your finished deck, pick a tone, and get ready-to-paste prompts that write a card-by-card booklet with the true story behind each reveal. Then check what comes back and turn it into a printable booklet. Everything runs in your browser.

How to use this tool

The idea in one minute

This tool does not write the booklet itself. It writes the prompts. You paste them into your own ChatGPT, ChatGPT writes the card-by-card booklet, and you paste the result back here to check it. Your deck data never leaves your browser.

Quick start

  1. Load deck. Import the project file saved from your deck app, or paste your cards. No deck yet? Press Load a 6-card example to see how everything works.
  2. Style. Pick the tone and the story length. Leave the rest on the defaults if you are unsure.
  3. Prompts. Open ChatGPT. Paste Prompt 1 and wait for the answer. Paste Prompt 2 in the same chat, and so on until the last one. Copy each answer into one document, in order.
  4. Check & export. Paste the whole booklet into the box and press Check booklet. Fix what the report flags, then download the booklet and the DATA_TO_VERIFY list.
  5. Printable booklet. Still in the same station: choose the page size, press Print or save as PDF and pick "Save as PDF". You get a finished booklet with a cover, one page per story and a closing note.
  6. Verify the facts. Check every flagged detail against a reliable source before you print or sell anything. VERIFY notes are left out of the printed booklet on purpose, so clear them first.

Tips for better stories

  • Write a clear reveal on every card. The story is built on it, so a good reveal gives a good story.
  • Add a source to each card. It travels with the prompt and into the DATA_TO_VERIFY file.
  • Use 4 to 6 cards per prompt. Smaller prompts give longer, more careful answers.
  • Not happy with the voice? Press Regenerate with a new angle and paste the new prompt in a fresh chat.
  • One story is off? Use Fix one card instead of redoing the whole booklet.

If something goes wrong

The check says cards are missing
ChatGPT stopped early. Write "Continue from CARD n" in the same chat, then paste the new part at the end of your booklet.
No entries were found
Each entry must start with CARD 1 | headline. Ask ChatGPT to follow the output format exactly.
The import says no cards found
Use the project file saved from your deck app, or paste your cards instead.
The copy button does nothing
Click inside the prompt box, select all and copy by hand.

Good to know

  • Your work is saved automatically in this browser. Clear deck removes it.
  • All four stations are always open. Each tool tells you what it still needs.
  • In station 4, the printable booklet can add a contents page, a Test yourself page with an answer key, a dedication and an about page. The page planner counts the pages and shows how to print a folded booklet.
  • AI can make mistakes. The check is a safety net, not a guarantee. Results vary.
