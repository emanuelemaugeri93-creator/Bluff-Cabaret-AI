Game Night Kit Forge

Pick your game world and your occasion, and get one ready-to-paste prompt that writes the extras for your game night: an invitation, a read-aloud opening script in the voices of your cast, house-rules cards and short round announcements. Then turn what comes back into printable pages. Everything runs in your browser.

How to use this tool

The idea in one minute

This tool does not write the kit itself. It writes the prompt. You paste it into your own ChatGPT, ChatGPT writes the kit, and you paste the result back here to check it and turn it into printable pages: an invitation card, an opening script, house-rules cards at card size and round announcement strips. Your details never leave your browser.

Quick start

  1. Your game. Choose the world of your deck and tick the characters who will speak. Add the game title. You can also import the project file saved from your deck app to fill it in.
  2. Occasion. Pick birthday, family night, classroom, team event or holiday party. Add only the details you really want in the invitation (host, date, place, how to reply). Anything you leave empty is never invented.
  3. Prompt. Choose the parts you want and the tone, then copy the prompt into ChatGPT. Not happy with the voice? Press Regenerate with a new angle and paste the new prompt in a fresh chat.
  4. Check & print. Paste the kit ChatGPT wrote and press Check kit. Fix what the report flags, then press Print or save as PDF and choose "Save as PDF". No kit yet? Press Try an example kit to see the printed pages.
  5. Run the night. Type who is coming, see the tables and the turn order, plan the timing with your own numbers, tick what you need and print the night pack with score sheets.
  6. Play. Cut the cards and strips, read the opening script out loud, send the invitation. Have fun.

Tips for a better kit

  • Tick 3 to 5 characters. Fewer voices make a tighter script, more voices make a bigger show.
  • Only fill in details you are sure about. The check flags any date, time, place or contact that ChatGPT adds by itself.
  • House rules are optional twists. They never change the scoring of your deck, so you can mix and match them.
  • Print the house-rules cards and the announcement strips on card stock, then cut along the dashed lines.
  • Read the opening script once before the guests arrive. It takes only a couple of minutes.

If something goes wrong

The check says a part is missing
Write "Now write the part called ..." in the same chat and paste the new part at the end.
No entries were found
Each part must start with its heading in capitals: INVITATION, OPENING SCRIPT, HOUSE RULES, ROUND ANNOUNCEMENTS. Ask ChatGPT to follow the output format exactly.
The copy button does nothing
Click inside the prompt box, select all and copy by hand.
A card text is too long
Ask ChatGPT to shorten that one rule or line, then paste the new text in place.

Good to know

  • Your work is saved automatically in this browser. Reset everything removes it.
  • All five stations are always open. Each tool tells you what it still needs.
  • AI can make mistakes. The check is a safety net, not a guarantee. Read everything before you send or print it. Results vary.
