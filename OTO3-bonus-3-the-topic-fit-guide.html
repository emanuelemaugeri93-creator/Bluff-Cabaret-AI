How to use the Topic Fit Guide

The idea in one minute

Both Engines turn sourced facts into a card game, but they ask different things of a topic. Odd One Out needs true statements that a convincing false statement can hide among. Trivia Duel needs facts with one clear, checkable answer. A topic that is great for one can be weak for the other. This guide helps you see the difference before you generate, so you do not spend image generations on a deck that was never going to work.

Quick start

  1. Skim 1 Fit criteria to see what each Engine wants.
  2. Open 3 Scan the banks, choose a world and add three or four promising topics to a shortlist.
  3. Open 4 Draft cards for each one and read the drafts: if you cannot make a good question or a convincing fake, the topic is weak.
  4. Run each shortlisted topic through 2 Test a topic. Keep the ones that score well and have no red flags.
  5. Work through 5 Checklist for the Engine you chose.
  6. Print the whole guide with the button at the top if you want a paper copy.

Good to know

  • The scan reads the wording of the sourced facts (numbers, years, names, cues of surprise). It gives a hint, not a verdict. Your own judgement comes first.
  • The true statements and correct answers come from sourced topic banks. The false statements and wrong options are written by ChatGPT, so check them before you print or sell.
  • Both Engines make Poker size cards, 63 x 88 mm, on A4 or US Letter.
  • Topic banks have 40 topics for each of the 5 core worlds and 20 for each of the 10 other worlds.

If something goes wrong

My topic is not in the banks
Type it in 2 Test a topic as your own topic. The questions work for any topic.
The scan ranks a topic I like low
It only reads the wording of three facts. If you know more facts, trust your test score instead.
The copy button does nothing
Select the text by hand and copy it.
I lost my shortlist
It is saved in this browser. Clearing your browser data removes it, so download it now and then.
