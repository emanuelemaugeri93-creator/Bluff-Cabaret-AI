The Launch Day Worksheet Pack

Seven worksheets you fill in on screen or on paper: pricing and margin (matching the Studio’s calculator), a launch day checklist with dates, first-batch tracking, a batch size planner, multi-deck offers, a reorder planner and a week 1 and week 4 review.   1. See it filled in. Load an example: a 20-card boxed deck launched ten days ago, with a first batch partly sold.
  2. Look around. Worksheet 1 gives profit and margin. Worksheets 4 and 5 test batch sizes and bundle prices. Worksheets 6 and 7 read the sales in worksheet 3.
  3. Make it yours. Clear the example and type your own costs in worksheet 1.
What the worksheets assume
  • Shipping is counted once per order. A 2-pack or 3-pack shares one shipping cost.
  • Stock left = units printed − units sold − gifts and samples − damaged or lost.
  • Selling speed uses only the sold rows that have a date. A few days of sales give a rough guide, not a forecast.
  • Every price, fee and cost is a number you type. Nothing here knows any price.
Example data is loaded. The names and numbers are invented to show how the tool works.
Read me first: how to use the worksheets

The idea in one minute

Launch day goes smoother when the numbers and the checks are written down before it. These three worksheets sit one under the other on this page. Fill them in on screen, then print them or save them as a PDF. You can also print them blank and fill them in by hand.

The worksheets only do arithmetic on the numbers you type. They do not know any prices, they do not predict sales and they do not promise any result.

Quick start

  1. Open Worksheet 1. Choose boxed game or printable download and type your own costs and your price.
  2. Work through Worksheet 2 and tick items as you finish them. Set your launch date to see the countdown.
  3. When you print a batch, enter it in Worksheet 3 and add a row each time units leave your hands.
  4. Use Worksheet 4 to compare print quantities from your quotes, and Worksheet 5 to test a bundle price.
  5. Once sales start, Worksheet 6 tells you when to reorder and Worksheet 7 helps you review week 1 and week 4.
  6. Use the bar at the bottom to jump between the worksheets and to print. In the checklist, Download the calendar gives you a file for your calendar app.

Tips

  • Use real quotes and real receipts. The tool can only be as accurate as your numbers.
  • Enter shipping and fees per order, and count every unit you give away or lose to a misprint.
  • If the profit per unit is negative, change the price or the costs and look again.
  • Test print on plain paper before you print the real batch.
