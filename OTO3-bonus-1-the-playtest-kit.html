How to use the Playtest Kit

The idea in one minute

A deck can look finished and still have weak cards: a false statement that is too easy to spot, a question with two right answers, a rule nobody understood. Players find these problems in minutes. This kit gives you a simple way to run the test, write down what you see, and turn it into a clear to-do list before you spend image generations or print anything.

Quick start

  1. Open 1 Set up the test, choose your game type and deck size, and write down who will play. Aim for two or three testers.
  2. Follow the session plan. Hand over the rulebook and do not explain more than it says.
  3. Keep 2 Log the cards open on a phone or tablet, or print the sheet from tab 5, and tap a row for each card you play.
  4. Note the setup time, the game length and the rules questions in tab 2.
  5. When the last card is done, fill in 3 Debrief.
  6. Open 4 What to fix first and work down the list from the top. Use the ChatGPT prompt of each item for the rewrite, then check the new text with reliable sources.
  7. Fix, save the session in 5 Rounds & decision, and test again. Release when the scorecard says you are ready.

Tips for an honest test

  • You are testing the deck, not the players. Say so out loud.
  • Do not hint, defend or explain during play. Every question a tester asks is useful data.
  • Watch faces more than cards: laughter, frowning and arguing tell you the most.
  • Test the card text before you make the artwork. Changing a line costs minutes, changing an image costs more.
  • Include one tester who does not usually play card games.

If something goes wrong

My deck has 40 or 72 cards
You do not have to log every card. Add only the cards you played, or use the quick-add buttons for a range.
Only two testers showed up
That is fine. Two honest testers beat a crowded table. Log the same way.
The fix list is empty
It only shows what you logged. Mark a row with an action (fix, replace, verify) or a reaction, and the item appears.
The copy button does nothing
Select the text by hand and copy it.

Always check the false statements and wrong options with reliable sources before you release a deck. AI can make mistakes. Your entries are saved in this browser.
