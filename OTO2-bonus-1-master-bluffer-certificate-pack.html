How to use this pack

The idea in one minute

A certificate is a small ceremony at the end of a game night. This pack gives you two ways to make one. Way 1: a prompt that makes ChatGPT create a fully illustrated certificate in the style of your deck's world. Way 2: a print-ready certificate built right here in your browser, in the colors of your world, with nothing to generate. Use either one, or both.

On the night: scores, script and prints

  1. Open 6 Winners and type the players and their points. Press Suggest the winners, then change any award by hand.
  2. Open 7 Ceremony script, choose a tone and read it out.
  3. Back in 6 Winners, press Print the winners' certificates. For small extras use 8 Badges.

Quick start with ChatGPT

  1. Set the Paper and the Orientation in the bar below. Add your game title and your shop or series name if you want them printed small.
  2. Open a tab and copy a prompt. Open your ChatGPT image chat, attach one approved card of your deck as a style reference, and paste the prompt.
  3. Read every word on the image. If a word is wrong, use Check & fix.
  4. Print at 100% (Actual size) on card stock and write the name, the date and the host by hand.

Quick start without ChatGPT

  1. Open 4 Print-ready. Choose a style, a world and an award.
  2. Type the names, one per line, and the date and host if you like.
  3. Press Print or save as PDF and choose "Save as PDF" in your print dialog.

Tips

  • Choose the same world as your deck. The prompts use its palette, lettering and finish.
  • Match the style to the audience: Noir for grown-up game nights, Family-friendly for kids.
  • Leave the name line blank on illustrated certificates. Handwriting is more reliable than generated names.
  • Keep the optional lines short. Less text means fewer spelling mistakes.
  • Print a test page on plain paper before you use card stock.

If something goes wrong

A word is misspelled on the image
Use 5 Check & fix to copy a correction prompt. Keep the same chat and the same reference image.
The page shape is wrong
The OUTPUT SIZE line is the first line of every prompt. Make sure it is still there when you paste.
The colors do not match my deck
Check the world in the menu, and attach a card from the deck as a reference.
The copy button does nothing
Click inside the prompt, select all and copy by hand.
