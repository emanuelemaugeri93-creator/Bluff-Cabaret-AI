How this works

  1. Pick a game and a world. The world sets the look of your cards.
  2. Your topics are chosen for you. Shuffle or change them if you like.
  3. Press Generate. Copy the card text prompt into ChatGPT.
  4. Paste ChatGPT's reply into Paste & Check. Fix anything flagged with the repair prompt.
  5. Copy the image script into ChatGPT, one prompt at a time, then print the rulebook, host sheet and fact sheet.

This tool only builds prompts and documents. It never creates images itself. Check the facts before you print or sell your deck. AI can make mistakes.
