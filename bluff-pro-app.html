    How to Use Bluff Cabaret AI

    1. Step 1 Setup: pick a world, a topic, players and game length, then copy the deck prompt into ChatGPT.
    2. Step 2 Paste & Check: paste ChatGPT's 24 cards back here, fix anything the Fact & Bluff Check and Game Balance flag.
    3. Step 3 Production Script: compile the ordered script and generate the images in ChatGPT top to bottom — style sheet first, then cast, then cards — keeping approved images as reference.
    4. Step 4 Print Kit: print, verify every flagged fact, disclose AI use where you sell.

    This app is a prompt engine: it never draws cards or images. All finished artwork is generated in ChatGPT from the prompts it compiles.

    What is new: pick a world with a recurring cast of 5 characters, play with fixed rules, hunt hidden Tell clues in every scene, then follow the ordered production script (style sheet → cast → cards → promo) so the printed deck looks like a premium collectible game.
