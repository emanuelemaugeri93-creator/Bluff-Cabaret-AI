How to use the Print Shop Brief

The idea in one minute

When you ask a printer for a quote, vague questions get vague answers. A clear brief says exactly what you want: card size, stock, finish, box, quantity and which files you are sending. This tool turns your answers into a one-page brief and a message you can paste, tells you which Launch Pack files to attach, and gives you a checklist of questions so nothing is forgotten before you order.

The tool does not name any printer, does not know any prices and does not promise that a printer will accept your files. It helps you ask well and compare fairly.

Quick start

  1. Export your Launch Pack from Boxed Game Studio (Station 6) so the files exist.
  2. Open 1 Spec table and fill in your game, quantities and choices. Leave a field empty if you do not know yet: the brief will show it as “to be agreed”.
  3. Open 2 Files to attach and tick the files you will send.
  4. Open 5 Brief & print, copy the message, attach the files and send it to the printers you chose.
  5. While you talk to them, use 3 Questions to ask. When the quotes arrive, enter them in 4 Compare quotes.
  6. Check each printer in 6 Fit check, and write to them with the messages in 7 Messages.
  7. Order a small test first. Print the alignment test on plain paper at home before you order anything.
  8. When the order arrives, work through 8 When it arrives.

Tips

  • Ask for the same specification from every printer. Only then are the quotes comparable.
  • The box template is a good starting point, especially for home printing and small runs. Many printers have their own templates: always check the size with the printer before you order.
  • Test print on plain paper first, at 100% scale, and measure a card: it should measure 63 x 88 mm.
  • Ask for a sample or a proof before a bigger order.
  • Keep the answers in writing. Tab 3 has a box for each one.

If something goes wrong

I do not know the stock weight
Leave it as “ask the printer”. Tab 3 has a question that asks which stocks they offer.
The printer wants single-card files
Ask what format they need. The Launch Pack has 9-up sheets with a back sheet for each; tell the printer exactly that.
My numbers look wrong in tab 4
Check that the unit price is per deck, not per card, and that the setup and shipping are one-time amounts.
The copy button does nothing
Select the text by hand and copy it.
